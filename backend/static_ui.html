STANDMAP

Standards Mapping & Applicability Platform (SIH26108)

Automated intelligence platform for identifying, verifying, and mapping applicable Indian Standards (IS) and mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs) for public procurement tenders.

The Problem in Public Procurement
  • Tenders often cite obsolete, withdrawn, or superseded IS standards (e.g. citing IS 1180:1989 instead of IS 1180:2014), causing litigation and re-tendering delays.
  • Procuring officers miss mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs) where ISI marks are compulsory by Central Government notifications.
  • Tender clauses specify vague parameters without identifying testing codes, safety rules, or insulation polymer classes.
The STANDMAP Solution
  • Source-grounded atomic requirement extraction with exact page and section provenance.
  • Deterministic physical subtype matching (e.g. distinguishing Monoset IS 9079 from Regenerative IS 8472).
  • Normative dependency graph linking primary product standards to mandatory test codes (e.g. IS 9652) and safety rules.
  • Zero hallucinations: missing specifications (like PVC vs XLPE cable) force human officer review instead of guessing.
